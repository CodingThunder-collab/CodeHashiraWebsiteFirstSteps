How to play

  • Jump with Space, Up or W, or tap the game. Duck with Down or S.
  • Ground limits need a jump, flying ones need a duck, and tall ones need a well-timed jump.
  • Grab coffee for slow motion and a 75% coverage shield to survive one hit. You also get three lives.
  • A new sprint starts every 30 seconds, and new limits unlock as you go. Chain dodges for a x2 and x3 combo.
  • The game pauses when you click away. Your best score and collection stay in this browser only.
Hashira's note: The game rounds a few corners, but the limits are real. If you've hit one of these in production, the Execution Governors and Limits page in the Apex Developer Guide has the exact numbers.
