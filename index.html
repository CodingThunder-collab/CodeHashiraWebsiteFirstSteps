TIP #001 LWC 30 Sep 2026 2 min read

Clean up LWC templates with lwc:if, lwc:elseif and lwc:else

Stop nesting if:true and if:false templates. LWC's lwc:if, lwc:elseif and lwc:else give you a flat, readable if / else-if / else chain.

Read the tip 
<!-- Before -->
<template if:true={isLoading}>
<template if:false={isLoading}>

<!-- After -->
<template lwc:if={isLoading}>
<template lwc:elseif={hasError}>
<template lwc:else>

Why "Code Hashira"?

Hashira (柱) is the Japanese word for pillar — the main post that holds up a traditional wooden house. Everything else rests on it.

Code Hashira is about becoming that pillar for your Salesforce team. Not with one giant leap, but one small, sharp tip at a time — shared freely with admins, developers and tech enthusiasts.
